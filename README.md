## How we pick

- New: first seen in the 7 days before Monday, and still open.
- Open to the country for sure: the post says, in its own words, that it hires people who live there. A post that names only some cities or states in a country isn't on its list.
- No recruitment agencies, gig platforms or reposts, and no company we are still checking. At most 2 jobs from one company.
- Jobs whose words name the country or its region first, then those that say anywhere; in each, those that state pay first, then the newest. Grouped by kind of work.

## From employers' own job boards

Every job here is on the employer's own careers site or applicant tracking system, not only on a job board or an aggregator. Each links to its page on firstmonday.ai, with the whole post and the employer's own link to apply.
